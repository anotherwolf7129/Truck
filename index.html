Cascade Heavy Haul — Anchor Point Terminal to Northgate

Eight miles across the city

Out of the port gate, six turns at signalised intersections, downtown, over Prospect Hill on a nine percent descent, up the loop ramp onto I‑118, off at exit 14 and out to the Northgate substation. Two police units run ahead and leapfrog the junctions, taking each light so the load never meets a red; a lead pilot car carries the height pole, and a chase car keeps everyone off your tail.

Pick what you are hauling. It changes everything — how many axles are under the load, how far the tail is behind you, how much of it the rear steer can save you at an intersection, and whether one engine can move it at all. Watch the rollover meter more than the speedometer, and gear down for the hill.
