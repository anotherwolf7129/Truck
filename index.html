Bennett Heavy Haul — Permit 24-0881-OS

400 MVA transformer to Cloverdale Substation

212,000 lb gross on a four-axle lowboy behind a jeep dolly. Twelve feet wide, which is wider than the lane you are driving in, so oncoming traffic has to get off the road. Two police units run ahead and leapfrog the junctions; a lead pilot car carries the height pole, and a chase car keeps everyone off your tail.

Seven and a half miles. There is a nine percent grade off the ridge — gear down and use the compression brake, because your service brakes will fade long before the bottom. The switchback needs the trailer's rear steer. Watch the rollover meter more than the speedometer.
